# Connected case design revision

Restores the original dark navy, blue and mint visual direction while retaining the larger typography and working enquiry backend.

The hero is now an interactive connected-case diagram: selectable information sources, a three-stage walkthrough, and a document-field population preview. Playback stops after the final stage and respects reduced motion.

A separate portfolio demo provides Management, Legal and Field perspectives, selectable pipeline bars, closure progress, and field photography. All figures are explicitly labelled sample data. Marketing copy stays concise, vendor-neutral, and focused on configuration and implementation.

Validation: production build, TypeScript in the clean Site checkout, browser checks for document population and finite playback, responsive views at 390px and 320px, mobile navigation, and team perspective selection. Existing enquiry storage and owner inbox are retained.
