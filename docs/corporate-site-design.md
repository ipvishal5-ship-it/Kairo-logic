# Kairologic Technologies corporate site

The company brand is Kairologic Technologies, confirmed by the owner on 27 October 2026. The case and workflow workspace is one product within the company website. The owner asked for large readable type, a coherent brand and research before implementation.

## Research

- Thoughtworks https://www.thoughtworks.com/ separates what the company does, how it works, company information and contact. This supports company navigation wider than one product demonstration.
- Work and Co https://work.co/ leads with a company statement and links specific work into deeper detail. This informs hierarchy, not its visual composition or copy.
- Linear https://linear.app/ grounds product explanation in concrete product views. The existing workflow walkthrough remains available on its own route.
- UI UX Pro Max https://github.com/nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill was queried for enterprise technology consultancy editorial design and readable typography. Swiss layout and contrast recommendations were considered; existing brand colors take precedence over its suggested palette.
- Antislop https://github.com/miqdadbadjuber/anti-slop is applied during the build at the owner's choice. Core, UI and copy guidance inform the work.
- Agent Reach https://github.com/Panniantong/Agent-Reach directs public research to native search and reading tools first. Official pages were available, so no channel installation or login was needed.

## Design read

B2B company website for business and technical decision makers, with a confident editorial visual language. ENERGY 2 RHYTHM 3 MOTION 1. Layouts vary with content; movement is limited to interaction feedback.

## Decisions and purpose

- Warm white and midnight blue create clear reading surfaces. Mint comes from the existing connected mark and is reserved for the branded composition and contact invitation.
- The existing connected K provides the identity motif. No new logo, stock team photography or unsupported proof points are introduced.
- Manrope has open counters and a broad clear shape suited to readable text. Body copy is 20 to 22 pixels, interface labels at least 16 pixels, and headings scale by screen size.
- An asymmetric opening gives the company statement the focal point; the enlarged mark gives the other half a brand role.
- One featured product receives a large split composition because it is the only confirmed product. No extra names or roadmap promises are invented.
- Capability rows explain the work without a repeated card template. The implementation sequence has four actual phases.
- A fixed light company theme makes editorial text and the blue identity consistent. The product demonstration retains its darker viewing surface.
- Contact remains a working form backed by the existing enquiry service, with labels, validation, loading, error and success feedback.
- Company, technologies, product detail, contact and privacy have real destinations. Header and footer carry only those destinations.
- Production browser QA exposed a Vinext RSC prefetch/navigation error in next/link. SiteLink renders an ordinary anchor so navigation works without that client router and also works without JavaScript.
